🔒 Privacy Policy

Information We Collect and Use

TRUSTBET collects and processes information necessary to provide and improve our services. This may include your name, email address, account details, verification information, device information, IP address, transaction history, Naira wallet activity, deposit and withdrawal details, betting activity, and customer support communications.

We may also collect information about how you access and interact with our website to help maintain service quality, prevent fraud, and protect your account.

Transaction history retention: All transaction history records, including deposits, bets, winnings, and withdrawals, are automatically cleared from the platform after 7 days. This scheduled cleanup does not change your wallet balance or delete your user account.

How We Use Your Information

TRUSTBET uses your information to:

  • Create, manage, and maintain your account.
  • Verify your identity and help prevent unauthorized access.
  • Process and manage deposit and withdrawal requests.
  • Maintain accurate wallet balances and transaction records.
  • Provide betting and gaming services and display account activity.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Detect suspicious activities, prevent fraud, and enforce platform rules.
  • Send important account updates, security alerts, and service notifications.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
Security and Contact

TRUSTBET takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information and account records against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Access to account information is restricted to authorized personnel and systems where necessary. Users are responsible for keeping passwords, verification codes, and login details confidential. TRUSTBET will never ask users to disclose passwords or one-time verification codes through unsolicited messages.

No online platform can guarantee absolute security. Report suspicious account activity immediately.
